# Wastage Reports

## Reports

- Wastage by user
- Wastage by roll
- Wastage by product
- Wastage by batch
- Shrinkage during reconciliation
- Over-cutting history

## Metrics

- Billed quantity
- Actual cut quantity
- Wastage quantity
- Estimated wastage cost

## Rules

Wastage report must be traceable to sale line, roll, user, and timestamp where applicable.
