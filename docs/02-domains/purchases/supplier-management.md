# Supplier Management

## Supplier Data

- Supplier name
- Phone
- Address
- Contact person
- Opening balance, pending confirmation
- Purchase history
- Outstanding payable balance

## Rules

- Supplier cannot be deleted if purchases exist.
- Supplier ledger must track payable changes.
- Supplier payments should reduce payable balance.
