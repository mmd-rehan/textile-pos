# Invoice Accounting

## Sales Invoice Impact

### Cash Sale

- Increase cash/payment account.
- Increase sales revenue.
- Deduct inventory through inventory movement.
- Recognize cost/profit if cost calculation is enabled.

### Credit Sale

- Increase customer receivable.
- Increase sales revenue.
- Update customer ledger.

## Rules

- Invoice accounting must be created transactionally with invoice completion.
- Invoice cancellation must create reversal/adjustment records, not silent deletion.
