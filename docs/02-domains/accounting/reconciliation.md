# Accounting Reconciliation

## Purpose

Accounting reconciliation ensures sales, cash, customer balances, supplier balances, and inventory financial impact are aligned.

## Potential Reconciliation Areas

- Daily sales vs cash collected
- Customer ledger vs invoices/payments
- Supplier ledger vs purchases/payments
- Inventory value vs roll stock
- Bank reconciliation, pending confirmation
