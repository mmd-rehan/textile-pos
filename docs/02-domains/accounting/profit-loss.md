# Profit and Loss

## Purpose

Profit/loss reporting should show revenue, cost, gross profit, expenses, and net profit.

## Confirmed Need

The project requires profit visibility and product-wise profit reporting.

## Calculation Areas

- Sales revenue
- Discounts
- Returns/refunds
- Cost of sold fabric/products
- Expenses
- Wastage/shrinkage impact, pending accounting policy

## Pending Confirmation

- Costing method for partial fabric sales.
- Whether landed cost is included in COGS.
- Whether wastage is treated as expense/loss or inventory adjustment only.
