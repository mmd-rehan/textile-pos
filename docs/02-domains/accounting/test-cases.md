# Accounting Test Cases

## Test Cases

1. Cash sale creates payment/cash entry.
2. Credit sale creates customer ledger entry.
3. Customer payment reduces outstanding balance.
4. Supplier purchase increases payable.
5. Supplier payment reduces payable, if enabled.
6. Expense reduces cash/bank balance, if cash management enabled.
7. Refund creates reversal entries.
8. Ledger adjustment requires permission and reason.
9. Profit report uses selected date range.
10. Financial records are not silently deleted.
