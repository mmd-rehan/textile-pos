# Cash Management

## Purpose

Cash management tracks cash inflow/outflow from sales, customer payments, supplier payments, expenses, and refunds.

## Rules

- Cash sale increases cash.
- Cash refund decreases cash.
- Cash expense decreases cash.
- Daily sales should reconcile with cash collected.

## Pending Confirmation

- End-of-day closing workflow.
- Cash drawer opening/closing balance.
- Cash handover between shifts.
