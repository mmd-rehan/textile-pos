# Customer Types

## Confirmed Customer Categories

- Retail customer
- Wholesale customer
- Credit customer

## Retail Customer

A walk-in or small-quantity customer. May or may not be saved in the system.

## Wholesale Customer

A customer purchasing in bulk, full rolls, multiple rolls, or large quantities. Usually requires purchase history and may need customer-specific pricing.

## Credit Customer

A customer allowed to buy now and pay later. Credit customers require ledger tracking, outstanding balance, payment history, and optionally credit limit.
