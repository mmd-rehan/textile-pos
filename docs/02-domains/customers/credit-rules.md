# Customer Credit Rules

## Credit Principles

Credit/Udhaar is important for textile wholesale and repeat customers.

## Proposed Rules Pending Confirmation

- Only approved customers can buy on credit.
- Credit limit may be optional.
- If credit limit is exceeded, system can block or require Manager/Admin approval.
- Partial payments are allowed.
- Payment allocation can be against oldest balance by default or manual invoice selection, pending confirmation.

## Required Tracking

- Outstanding balance
- Payment history
- Credit sales
- Due reminders, if enabled
- Credit limit, if enabled
