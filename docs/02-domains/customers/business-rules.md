# Customer Business Rules

## Rules

1. Customer ledger must show real-time outstanding balance.
2. Customer payments must create ledger entries.
3. Credit sale must increase outstanding balance.
4. Customer payment must decrease outstanding balance.
5. Customer-specific pricing should be supported for wholesale once pricing policy is confirmed.
6. Customer deletion should be restricted if transactions exist.
7. Customer phone should be searchable.
