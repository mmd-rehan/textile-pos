# Customer Validation Rules

## Validation Rules

- Customer name is required.
- Phone should be optional or required based on confirmed business rule.
- Credit limit must be zero or positive.
- Credit cannot be enabled without required fields once policy is confirmed.
- Payment amount must be greater than zero.
- Ledger adjustment requires reason.
