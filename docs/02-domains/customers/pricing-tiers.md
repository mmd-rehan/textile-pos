# Customer Pricing Tiers

## Purpose

Pricing tiers allow different customers to receive different sale rates.

## Potential Tier Examples

- Retail
- Wholesale
- VIP wholesale
- Special negotiated customer

## Rules Pending Confirmation

- Tier may define default discount or price list.
- Product-specific customer pricing may override tier pricing.
- Manual POS price override may still be permitted with permission.
