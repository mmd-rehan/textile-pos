# Discount Rules

## Discount Types

Potential discount types:

- Line-level fixed amount
- Line-level percentage
- Invoice-level fixed amount
- Invoice-level percentage
- Customer-specific negotiated rate

## Rules

- Discounts must be stored explicitly.
- Discounts must not alter inventory deduction.
- Discount permission should be role-based.
- Discount reason may be required above a configured threshold.
