# Terminology

## Thaan / Roll

A fabric roll that contains a measurable fabric length.

Example:
- 30 yards roll

---

## Roll Length

Original measured length of a fabric roll.

---

## Remaining Length

Current remaining usable fabric in a roll.

---

## Remnant / Chant

Small leftover fabric piece remaining after multiple sales.

Usually discounted.

---

## Batch / Dye Lot

Production batch of fabric.

Different batches may have slight color variation.

---

## Actual Cut

Actual physically cut fabric length.

---

## Billed Quantity

Quantity charged to customer.

---

## Wastage

Difference between:
- actual cut
- billed quantity

Example:
- billed = 3 yards
- actual = 3.2 yards
- wastage = 0.2 yards

---

## Roll Retirement

Process of marking a roll as finished.

---

## Wholesale Customer

Customer purchasing in bulk quantities.

---

## Retail Customer

Walk-in or small quantity customer.

---

## Credit Customer

Customer allowed to pay later.

---

## Dead Stock

Inventory not sold for a long period.

---

## Shrinkage

Inventory loss due to:
- cutting errors
- physical loss
- damage
- mismanagement

---

## Landed Cost

Actual cost including:
- purchase
- transport
- taxes
- labor

---

## Ledger

Financial transaction history for customer/supplier.

---

## Inventory Adjustment

Manual stock correction process.
