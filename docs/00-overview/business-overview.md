# Business Overview

## Industry

Textile Trading & Fabric Retail

---

# Business Type

The business operates in both:

- Retail textile sales
- Wholesale textile trading

---

# Products Sold

## Variable-Length Fabric

Examples:
- Wash & Wear
- Cotton
- Lawn
- Silk
- Palachi

These are sold in:
- yards
- meters

Inventory decreases dynamically.

---

## Cut Suit Pieces

Examples:
- 3-piece suits
- kids suits
- ready-cut suits

These may:
- come directly from suppliers
- or be cut from rolls

---

## Fixed Products

Examples:
- Kashmiri shawls
- scarves
- blankets
- accessories

These use normal quantity inventory.

---

# Inventory Nature

Inventory is primarily roll-based.

Example:

1 roll may contain:
- 30 yards
- 29.5 yards
- 32 yards

Sales happen in variable measurements.

---

# Major Operational Challenges

## 1. Variable-Length Sales

Customers buy different lengths:
- 2.75 yards
- 3.5 meters
- 5 yards

Traditional POS systems do not manage this accurately.

---

## 2. Wastage & Over-Cutting

Salesmen may:
- accidentally cut extra fabric
- bill less than actual cut

This creates hidden inventory loss.

---

## 3. Batch Consistency

Different fabric batches may have slight color differences.

Batch tracking is important.

---

## 4. Remnants

Small leftover fabric pieces must be tracked properly.

---

## 5. Credit Sales

Wholesale and repeat customers often purchase on credit.

---

# Business Objectives

The business wants:
- accurate inventory tracking
- fast billing
- wastage control
- profit visibility
- customer management
- supplier management
- business analytics

---

# Operational Priorities

Priority order:

1. Inventory accuracy
2. Billing speed
3. Wastage control
4. User accountability
5. Reporting
6. Scalability

---

# Business Workflow Summary

Purchase → Roll Entry → Barcode Assignment → Inventory Storage → Retail/Wholesale Sale → Inventory Deduction → Accounting → Reporting
