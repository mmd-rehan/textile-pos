# User Roles

## Admin

Full system access.

Responsibilities:
- manage settings
- manage users
- inventory control
- reporting
- accounting access
- role management

---

## Manager

Operational management access.

Responsibilities:
- inventory monitoring
- sales oversight
- purchase approvals
- customer management
- reporting

Restrictions:
- limited system configuration access

---

## Cashier / Salesman

POS-focused access.

Responsibilities:
- retail billing
- wholesale billing
- barcode scanning
- customer handling

Restrictions:
- cannot modify inventory directly
- cannot access accounting reports

---

## Inventory Staff

Inventory-only access.

Responsibilities:
- roll entry
- barcode printing
- stock adjustment
- roll reconciliation

Restrictions:
- no financial access

---

## Accountant

Accounting-focused access.

Responsibilities:
- ledger management
- payments
- expenses
- reconciliation
- financial reports

Restrictions:
- no inventory modification

---

## Super Admin (Optional)

Multi-branch administrative role.

Responsibilities:
- branch management
- global reporting
- enterprise configuration
